# SafeTrail Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

SafeTrail is a student safety app. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## What the app stores

- **Trusted contacts**: the names and phone numbers you add. They are stored only on your phone.
- **Journey details**: your destination, expected arrival time and last known location, stored on your phone and deleted when the journey ends.

## What the app uses and why

- **Location**: used while a journey is active or when you trigger an SOS, so your trusted contacts can see where you are. SafeTrail shares it only as a map link inside an SMS to the contacts you chose.
- **SMS**: used to send alerts to your trusted contacts, only when you miss a check-in or trigger an SOS.
- **Notifications**: used to ask "Are you safe?" and to show that a journey is running.
- **Motion sensor**: used on the home screen to detect a shake for SOS. Sensor data is not stored or sent anywhere.

## What the app does not do

- It has no account, no sign-up and no server.
- It does not upload your contacts, location or any other data to the developer or to anyone else.
- It has no advertising and no analytics.

## Your control

- You can delete any trusted contact at any time in the Contacts tab.
- You can end a journey at any time with "I've arrived safely".
- You can withdraw any permission in your phone's app settings, or delete the app to remove all stored data.
